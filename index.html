Privacy Policy

Effective: January 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: April 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you register or use VyCheckout — including your name, email address, business name, and payment details. We also automatically collect usage data, device information, IP addresses, and transaction records to operate and improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process payments and send transaction confirmations
  • To manage your account and provide customer support
  • To calculate and credit referral commissions accurately
  • To detect fraud, prevent abuse, and ensure platform security
  • To send important service updates and notices
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with trusted service providers — PayPal for payment processing and Supabase for secure data storage — under strict confidentiality agreements. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Payment Data

All payment processing is handled by PayPal. VyCheckout does not store card numbers or full payment credentials. PayPal's own privacy policy governs all data collected at the point of payment. You can review it at paypal.com/privacy.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential session cookies for authentication and to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function correctly.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and profile data for as long as your account remains active. Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 7 years to comply with financial and accounting regulations. You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@vycheckout.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) for all data in transit and apply strict access controls to data at rest. While we take security seriously, no system can guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login credentials private.

9. Children's Privacy

VyCheckout is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Users

VyCheckout operates globally. By using our platform, you consent to your information being processed and stored in servers that may be located outside your country of residence. We apply the same level of data protection regardless of where data is processed.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our platform. Continued use after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@vycheckout.com.
